# Car personality

## Purpose
Bits of personality for each car: one small thing each that only that car does. They are cosmetic (seen and heard).

## Requirements

### Requirement: Tornado Shark starts dusty
Tornado Shark is rarely used, so at the start of a race it is assumed to be dusted: the takeoff and a couple of turns SHALL make the car lose a bit of dust. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: the first corners
- **WHEN** Tornado Shark starts a race and takes its first turns
- **THEN** dust comes off it at the takeoff and in the first couple of turns, and none later

### Requirement: Lead Oxide shows heat at the front
On Lead Oxide, heat SHALL come from the front at times. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: driving it hard
- **WHEN** Lead Oxide is driven hard
- **THEN** heat shows at its front now and then

### Requirement: Kool Kat lands heavily
When Kool Kat lands on all four wheels, its suspension SHALL lower a bit, with a small thonk sound. Merely cosmetic: it is assumed to be a heavy car. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: a landing on all four
- **WHEN** Kool Kat lands on all four wheels
- **THEN** its body dips and comes back, and a small thonk is heard

### Requirement: Mighty Eight breathes and burns big
On Mighty Eight the radiator SHALL blow light particles when the gas is released, and nitro SHALL have a bigger visual effect. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: lifting off, then nitro
- **WHEN** Mighty Eight lifts off the gas, and later burns a nitro
- **THEN** light particles blow from its radiator on the lift, and its nitro flame is bigger than other cars'

### Requirement: MAX Revenge's turbocharger moves
On MAX Revenge, strong throttle SHALL make the turbocharger move, like a slight jigglebone. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: full throttle
- **WHEN** MAX Revenge goes to full throttle
- **THEN** its turbocharger visibly shakes a little

### Requirement: M A S H E E N's plow carries dirt
M A S H E E N's plow SHALL pick up a small wedge of dirt. Take a jump and pitch the nose down, and two or three chunky polygons tumble out. Dirt can be carried from off-road and dumped onto clean asphalt half a lap later. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: carrying dirt
- **WHEN** M A S H E E N drives off-road, then jumps over asphalt with its nose pitched down
- **THEN** a wedge of dirt sits in its plow, and two or three chunks tumble out onto the asphalt and stay there

### Requirement: DR Monstaa's tyres squash
When DR Monstaa lands on one wheel, that tyre SHALL visibly squash: for an instant one huge tyre is flattened against the ground while the opposite one is still round in the air. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: a one-wheel landing
- **WHEN** DR Monstaa lands on one wheel
- **THEN** that tyre is drawn flattened for a moment and the others stay round

### Requirement: Something slides in Wow Caninaro
In Wow Caninaro something loose SHALL slide across the luggage area on hard corners. Two left turns in succession produce only one knock; turn right and it is heard travelling all the way back. What is rolling around in there stays a mystery. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: left, left, right
- **WHEN** Wow Caninaro corners hard left twice, then right
- **THEN** one knock is heard for the two lefts, then a slide and a knock on the right

### Requirement: EL KING's cab rocks
After a hard stop, EL KING's cab SHALL rock forward. On releasing the brake there is one short breath of compressed air as the cab settles back, especially audible when EL KING pulls up behind the player. Like a jigglebone. (Owner, 2026-11-26.)

#### Scenario: Check: a hard stop
- **WHEN** EL KING stops hard, then its brake is released
- **THEN** its cab rocks forward and settles back, with one short breath of compressed air on the release
