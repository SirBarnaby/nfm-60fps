## Context

A car in the game is a rigid body on four wheel points (`Madness.drive`): the wheels are placed from the car's middle, pitch (`pzy`) and roll (`pxy`) and heading (`car.xz`); the ground and the track's collision boxes push them; the car's middle and angles are worked out again from where they end. Each wheel has a velocity (`scx`, `scy`, `scz`), and every wheel moves by the mean of the four, so the car moves as one. On the ground the velocities are pulled toward the velocity the car's speed and heading ask for (`speed` along `car.xz`), each axis by at most `grip` a tick: a square, not a circle. The grip is the car's own (`stat.grip`, 18 to 27), less while the heading turns fast and with the handbrake, less on grass and dirt. Turning is the heading itself (`car.wxz / steerDivisor` degrees a tick); a slide is the velocity lagging behind it. The body leans in corners by `tilt`, the whole car (wheels too) rotated. The race ticks every 53 ms; physics and AI use the game's own integer-degree sines and `StrictMath`.

Race extras (the powerups' change) tick inside `advanceRace` after every car drove, keep their state in one `sync(RaceInts)` that `RaceSnapshot` saves and restores and `RaceState` hashes as `extras` only while one plays, and a recording names those that played.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- The requirement's parts, behind the `handling` switch, for every car (the computer cars drive on the same tyres): springs and dampers at each wheel, one grip shared between braking and turning with a smooth curve, weight moving, a drift assist; 120 steps a second for those.
- It stays the game: the steering keys, gears, top speeds, air control, stunts, collisions, damage and the cars' own grip and bounce numbers are the game's; the change is at the tyres and in how the body is drawn.
- With the switch off, `Madness.drive` runs as written: the tyre hook returns `-1F` before touching anything, the lean is the game's.
- Run-ahead, rollback and the verifier cover it: all its state is in `sync`.
- No allocation per tick or frame once the race starts.

**Non-Goals:**
- Stepping the whole race 120 times a second: every number in `drive`, the computer drivers and the checkpoints is per 53 ms tick, in whole degrees. That is the rebuild after 1.0 (`release-1-0`); this change reports it as the gap in the first check.
- Recorded races with the handling (`test\races-extras`): they wait for the testing harness the owner will specify.
- Retuning the computer drivers for the new feel.

## Decisions

1. **Two halves, like the powerups.** `HandlingRace` is the race part, plain arrays changed only inside the race tick (the tyre hook in `drive` and `Feature.raceTick`); `HandlingFeature` holds the switch, delegates the race calls and draws the body from what `HandlingRace` holds after real ticks, never writing it.
2. **Where it acts.** One hook in `drive`'s grip block, after the game has worked out the velocity it wants (`targetX/Y/Z`) and its grip for the tick: `FeatureBridge.tyres(...)` copies the wheels' velocities, the targets, the grip, speed, heading, last heading (`txz`), pitch, roll and wheel layout into a `Chassis`, the extra moves the velocities and may turn the heading, and they are copied back. It returns the grip to show (lower while the tyres slide, so the game's own dust, skid sounds and the skidmarks extra follow the slide), or `-1F` when the extra does not play; then the game's own per-axis pull runs as it always did. The upward pull (`scy`) stays the game's, with the game's grip. A second test, `FeatureBridge.springs()`, leaves out the game's `tilt` while the springs show the lean.
3. **120 steps a second.** A count of the extra's ticks `t`; tick `t` takes `((t+1)·159)/25 − (t·159)/25` steps (6 or 7; 159 in 25 ticks = 120 a second of 53 ms ticks). Each step is `0.053/n` seconds. The springs and the tyres step together, so the tyres feel each step's loads.
4. **Springs.** Each wheel's compression `x` (units, up to 16 either way) and its speed `u` (units a second): `u += (ω²(x* − x) − 2ζωu)·dt; x += u·dt`, stopped at the ends. Per car: `f = 1.9 − 1.6(bounce − 1)` Hz (1.4 to 1.85 for the game's 1.05 to 1.3) and `ζ = 0.42 − 0.4(bounce − 1)`: the cars that leaned most in the game ride softest. The rest point `x*`: `0.5·b·(−a)` forward (a: the change of speed this tick; braking loads the front) plus `0.55·b·(speed·Δheading)` to the outside (the corner's sideways pull), `b` the car's bounce. In the air the springs hang 5 units long. Kicks: a landing after two ticks in the air adds `3.2 × fall` (units a tick, at most 60) to every spring's speed; while on the ground the ground pitching or rolling under the car by `d` degrees (at most 20) in a tick kicks each spring by `0.35 × (its lever × d in radians) / 0.053`, so the body lags behind the ground and settles.
5. **Load.** A wheel's load is `1 + x/18` (0.15 to 2.2). An axle's grip is the mean of `load^0.8` over its wheels (a tyre grips less per load the more it carries, so moving weight costs a little grip overall).
6. **Tyres: one circle, a smooth curve.** All four wheels share one grip `g = grip × 1.1 × (front + rear)/2` (1.1: the circle's grip matches the game's square on average). Each step, each wheel's slip `d = target − velocity` (on the road's plane) is pulled in by `f·h` (h: the step's share of the tick), `f = g·sin(1.5·atan(√3/0.7 · |d|/g))`: linear for a small slip, the most at `0.7g`, falling to 71% of it in a full slide (the magic formula's shape with C = 1.5). Braking and turning are one slip, so they share one grip. The fall past the peak is what lets a slide be held: on the throttle with the nose held into it, the tyres stay past their peak. One grip for all four keeps the four velocities together, as the game's pull does; per-wheel grips would set the game's wall-turn term (`forca`) turning the car by a sign left from the last wall it hit.
7. **The balance.** The axles' grips turn the car: the heading's own change this tick (`car.xz − txz`, the steering) times `0.5 × (front − rear)` more. Braking into a corner (front loaded) turns in more; power out of it (rear loaded) a little less. Only forwards above 20.
8. **Drift assist.** When the velocity points more than 38 degrees from the nose (forwards, above 20, the car moving faster than 30), the nose turns toward it by 60% of the excess each tick; steering into the slide turns it 25% more. Together at most 12 degrees a tick. What is left of a degree waits for the next tick (the heading is whole degrees).
9. **The lean.** The game's `tilt` rotates the whole car, wheels too; with the springs the body is drawn over its wheels instead, so the game's `tilt` is left out (its arithmetic unchanged when the extra is off). The race itself has no body lean then: a car's collision shape is its rigid one.
10. **The body drawn on its springs.** After each real tick `HandlingFeature` works out each car's body pose from the springs: pitch `atan((rear − front)/wheelbase)` (at most 7 degrees), roll `atan((right − left)/track)` (8), lowering 70% of the mean compression. A new hook in `Plane.d`, beside the bent wheels', asks the extra (`Features.carBody`, through a `CarBody` interface, one owner like `CarLook`) for each of the car's body polygons (not its wheels'): rolled and pitched about the axles' height and lowered, so the wheels stay on the road while the body moves over them. Drawn between ticks at the frame's share of the way from the tick before, as the cars move; the kept scene is recorded once a tick, so with the graphics card's projected scenes the body moves in tick steps (no change to the card's shaders here).
11. **State.** `HandlingRace.sync` holds the springs (`x`, `u` for every wheel), each car's last speed, last pitch and roll, ticks in the air, last fall, the degree left over, whether the tyres ran this tick, and the tick count. Set up from nothing when the stage loads.
12. **Determinism.** `StrictMath` for every sine, arc tangent, power and root in the race part; the game's own `RadicalMath` is not used there (its whole degrees are too coarse for a step). No dice.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The computer drivers were tuned to the game's grip] → the circle grips as the square on average and the drift assist caps slides; they may brake late into some corners. Their retuning is after 1.0 with the rest.
- [The first check is not met] → reported at the top of the report with its numbers: the springs and tyres step 120 times a second, the race around them 18.9.
- [The body steps once a tick with the graphics card's kept scenes] → small at these springs (a few degrees a second at most); a body pose in `ProjectedScene` and its shader (with the projection test) is the follow-up.
- [Untested] → per the owner's note no gate runs; `HandlingCheck` is written for `test\extras-check.ps1 handling`, run when testing resumes.
