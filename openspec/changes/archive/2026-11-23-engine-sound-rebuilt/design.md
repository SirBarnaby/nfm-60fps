## Context

The 3D sound (`Sound3D`, OpenAL Soft with Steam Audio) plays each car's engine as the five recordings the game has for its engine, all looping at once and mixed by the car's speed, throttle, air and wheelspin (`EngineVoices.mix`), each bent in pitch by OpenAL; a skid is a one-shot clip at the car, posted from inside the race tick. The recordings are short loops at 8000 samples a second, a few engine cycles each (the idle one about 15 cycles of 48 ms, the rising ones about 8 cycles of 64 to 82 ms). The sound thread updates a hundred times a second, carrying positions on between ticks; the game publishes a `Moment` once a tick. The extras framework copies the race after each tick into a `RaceView`, with each wheel's place, contact, surface and slide (`slipSide`, `slipLong`), and the skidmarks share the slide out over the wheels (`SkidSlip`).

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** the requirement "The improved sound is rebuilt from the game's own recordings", each scenario checked as far as a check can (the listening waits for the owner); only the 3D sound changes, and only for cars the switch plays; Classic sound and the 3D sound with the switch off exactly as before; nothing allocated per update or tick by the new code; the race never read from the sound thread.

**Non-Goals:** new recordings or a physical engine model (approaches B and C, not picked); changing which recording plays when (the 3D sound's mix stays: it is what the game's own sound means); the instant replay's wheels (a replay keeps the cars' places, not their slides: no skids in replays, as before).

## Decisions

1. **One cycle at a time.** Each recording is cut into its cycles once, when its engine is first heard: the period is the lag of the loop's strongest autocorrelation between 2 ms and half the loop (the shortest lag within 5% of the best, so a run of two cycles is not taken for one), refined between samples. A voice plays the recording as grains: each grain is one cycle with its neighbours' halves under a raised-cosine window, centred where the next firing falls. To raise the revs the grains are laid closer (pitch-synchronous overlap-add), not played faster, so the cycle's own sound (its tone, its "formants") stays and only the firing rate rises; lowering them lays the grains further apart with windows as wide as the gap, so no silence opens. The level is kept by the window's overlap. Successive grains walk the loop's cycles in order, with now and then a step to another cycle of the loop, so the sound breathes like a running engine instead of repeating a loop. Synthesized at 16000 samples a second (the recordings read between their samples), in 20 ms blocks, four kept queued (80 ms ahead, six in the sound test's lockstep, which mixes 100 ms at a time).
2. **The mix stays the 3D sound's.** Which recordings play, how loud and at what pitch is `EngineVoices.mix`, smoothed as before; for a rebuilt car the pitch becomes each voice's firing rate instead of OpenAL's playback speed. Far cars play their loudest recording only, as before.
3. **Front and back.** The voices' sum is shaped twice. The front, the engine and the intake: the lowest end thinned (a high-pass at 120 Hz), the intake's resonance (a band at 700 Hz) louder the more the throttle is open. The back, the exhaust: the body below a kilohertz (a low-pass), fuller and softly clipped under load (a growl, the clipping's harmonics), and pops when the throttle lifts at high revs: for 0.8 s after a lift above half the band, short decaying bursts at random, a few a second. Each plays from a streamed source of its own: the front at the car's nose (the model's front, a little in, at the bonnet's height), the back at its tail (the model's back, low). Both are placed by the car's pose (yaw, pitch, roll) and carried on between ticks with the car, muffled by what is in the way and dulled off the throttle as the engines are, and bent by Doppler.
4. **Skids per wheel.** After each tick the extra works out how hard each wheel slides, from the skidmarks' per-wheel slide (`SkidSlip.slide`: the front wheels in understeer, the rear in a power slide or under the handbrake, the outside wheels harder, a wheel off the ground nothing): silent below 4 units a tick, full at 24, and whether the ground under it is road (the skid recordings) or loose (the dust skid ones). Each wheel has a streamed source at the wheel (the model's hub, down to the tread), playing the original skid recording in overlapping 80 ms grains from its steady middle, chosen at random, so it lasts as long as the slide with no seam; its level follows the slide (20 ms up, 120 ms down) and its pitch rises a little with it. A wheel that rolls makes no skid sound. The car's one-shot skids (`carSkid`) are left out while the extra plays it.
5. **Where the data goes.** The extra computes the wheels' skids in its `afterTick` (the game thread, real ticks only). `GameSparker.publishSound`, which runs after the extras' tick, fills each car's pose and, through `FeatureBridge`, whether the extra plays it and its four skids, as plain fields of the `Car` it already makes (no new objects). The car's body (its front, back and wheels in its own frame) is handed to the sound once when a race starts (`Sound3D.carBody`) into arrays made once. A replay or highlight after the race keeps the body and plays the rebuilt engine with no skids.
6. **Switching.** The switch can be turned during a race: a car the extra stops playing fades back to the five recordings, one it starts playing fades them out; the streamed sources are paused while silent. Their random choices (which cycle next, the pops) come from a generator of their own, seeded in the sound test's lockstep, so the sound test stays repeatable.

## Performance

Per car heard: two voices of one to three recordings, each a few grains of a few multiplications a sample, at 16000 samples a second, and a skid voice per sliding wheel: a few hundred thousand multiplications a second for a full grid, on the sound thread. Windows come from a table. Grains, blocks and filters are made once per car; nothing is allocated after a car is first heard.

## Classic and the switch off

Classic sound never reaches `Sound3D`. With the switch off `Car.rebuilt` is false and every path is the one before; no random number is drawn from the 3D sound's own generator by the new code. Hidden runs pin the switch off, so `verify-sound` renders as before.
