# Need For Madness: LIT, the extras' car sheets (realism, "Each car has a short sheet").
# The Damage and heat extra reads them: where steam and smoke come from, how hot
# the brakes run, how the exhaust behaves.
#
# One line per car: its model name, then
#   engine    the engine's layout: I4, I6, V6, V8, V10, V12, flat4, flat6, rotary
#   sits      where the engine sits: front, mid or rear
#   cooling   radiator at the front, radiators in the side pods, or air-cooled: front, sides, air
#   turbo     yes or no (a supercharger counts as yes)
#   brakes    drum, disc or carbon
#   exhausts  1 or 2 pipes at the back, or stacks (upright pipes behind the cab)
# and after them, for the cars that have one, traits written name=value:
#   spikes=yes      the car has spikes: a glint and sparks whenever it rams another car
#   charger=blower  what its "turbo yes" is: turbo (the default: a whistle that spools up, a blow-off
#                   when the throttle lifts) or blower (a supercharger: a whine that follows the revs)
#   pipes=side      its exhaust comes out at the side, so it is heard from there, not from the tail
#   beeps=yes       a truck: it beeps while it reverses
#   pops=yes        its exhaust pops when the throttle lifts at high revs, each pop heard and seen as
#                   a flash at a pipe (the default for "turbo yes"; pops=no for one that does not)
#   loud=1.4        how far its engine carries, 1 for most (0.5 to 1.6): a strong engine is heard from
#                   further off, a quiet car is harder to place by ear; up close all are as loud
#   revs=6          how far its engine's pitch climbs through a gear, in semitones a third of the
#                   gear (4 for most; a screamer more, a truck less)
#   pipe=own        its model has exhaust pipes of its own; every other car is fitted with drawn
#                   ones where its exhaust ends (out of the tail, along the sills, or as stacks)
#
