The 3D sound muffles cars behind the track and adds the track's reverb with
Steam Audio, shipped as dlls/win64/phonon.dll (version 4.8.1, Valve's own
build from the release at https://github.com/ValveSoftware/steam-audio,
unmodified, loaded at run time through Java's foreign function API):

  Steam Audio   https://valvesoftware.github.io/steam-audio/   Apache License 2.0

The full license text: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Steam Audio's third-party notices are in its repository.
Without the DLL the 3D sound plays on, without muffling or reverb.
